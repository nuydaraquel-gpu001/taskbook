Privacy Policy

Taskbook is a student task organizer built by [Your Name] as a school project. This policy explains what information Taskbook collects, why, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and password. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us. You can also give a display name. It is optional and is only used to greet you. If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares your name, email address and profile photo link with Taskbook’s Firebase project.
  • Your tasks. For each task you add, we store its title, subject, due date, priority, any steps (subtasks) you add, whether it is done, when it was created, and when you completed it. We also store the total minutes you spent on it with the focus timer.
  • Your preferences. If you save preferences on the My Account page, we store your daily goal, focus and break lengths, default sort order, and whether the timer sound and “hide finished tasks” options are on.
  • Technical information. Firebase Authentication records IP addresses and browser information (user agents) to run sign-in and prevent abuse. GitHub Pages, which hosts this website, logs visitors’ IP addresses for security.

We do not ask for your school, phone number, location or any other personal details.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To send account emails you ask for or need: a link to confirm your email address when you sign up or request one, and password reset links. Firebase sends these on our behalf. We never send marketing emails.
  • To save your tasks and show them on any device where you sign in.
  • To show your own progress: your daily goal, completion streaks, tasks finished, and focus time. These are calculated from your tasks in your browser and are only shown to you.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers listed below that run the website.

Where your data is stored

  • Firebase (Google). Your account is managed by Firebase Authentication and your tasks are stored in Cloud Firestore. Both are Google services. See the Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Fonts. The website’s font is loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when the page loads. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • GitHub Pages. The website files are hosted by GitHub. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Security rules on the database allow each signed-in user to read and change only their own tasks and preferences. Other users cannot see them.

Focus timer, downloads and undo

The focus timer runs in your browser. When you finish a full focus session, Taskbook adds that session’s minutes to the task’s focus time in your account.

Export to calendar and Download my data create files on your own device. Taskbook does not send them anywhere, and what happens to them afterwards is up to you.

When you delete a task, a copy stays in your open browser tab for a few seconds so you can press Undo. If you don’t, the copy is discarded.

Cookies and browser storage

Taskbook does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you across other websites. To keep you signed in, Firebase Authentication saves a sign-in token in your browser’s storage. Signing out removes it. If you use “Continue with Google”, Google may set its own cookies during sign-in.

How long we keep it

Your tasks and account stay until you delete them. According to Firebase, logged IP addresses are kept for a few weeks, and deleted account data is removed from its backup systems within 180 days.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete any task at any time.
  • On the My Account page, you can:
    • change or remove your display name
    • change your password
    • update your preferences
    • use Download my data to get a copy of your account details, preferences and tasks
    • use Delete all my tasks to remove every task in your account
    • use Delete my account to permanently delete your account, tasks and preferences
  • Use Forgot your password? on the sign-in page to reset your password.

Children and students

Taskbook is meant for students. It collects only what is needed to sign in, save tasks and remember your preferences. If you are under the age where your country requires a parent’s consent to use online services, please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
