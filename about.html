About Taskbook

A calm, simple planner made for students.

Taskbook helps you keep track of every assignment, project and reading in one place. You can see what's due today, plan the week ahead, and focus on one task at a time. It's free, it works on your phone and computer, and your tasks are private to your account.

Open Taskbook See the features

Why Taskbook?

Students juggle many subjects at once, and deadlines are easy to lose in notebooks, group chats and memory. Taskbook brings them together, shows what needs attention first, and turns big assignments into small steps you can actually finish.

What you can do

Organize assignments

Add tasks with a subject, due date and priority. Filter, search and sort them in seconds.

Break tasks into steps

Split big projects into small steps and watch the progress bar fill up as you go.

Focus timer

Work in focused sessions with short breaks. Taskbook keeps count of the minutes you spend on each task.

Plan your week

See how busy the next 7 days are, and export due dates to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook.

Goals and streaks

Set a daily goal and build a streak by finishing at least one task every day.

Sync on every device

Sign in on your phone, laptop or a school computer and your tasks are already there.

Getting started

  1. Create an accountSign up with your email or continue with Google. It takes less than a minute.
  2. Add your assignmentsType what's due, pick the subject and due date, and set a priority.
  3. Work through your listStart a focus session, check off steps, and mark tasks done to keep your streak going.

Your privacy

Your tasks can only be seen by you. Taskbook has no ads, doesn't sell your data, and doesn't track you. You can download or delete everything at any time from My Account.

How it's built

Taskbook is written in HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

  • HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • GitHub Pages

Firebase handles sign-in and stores your tasks. GitHub Pages hosts the website.
